Overview

Survive escalating waves of zomnics, unlock buyable areas, connect power, and roll the Mystery Box for stronger kits. Bosses gate key progression. Coordination, route knowledge, and perk economy determine late‑game survival.

  • Players: 1–4 Co‑op
  • Goals: Survive waves • Open routes • Complete boss checks • Optimize perks
  • Economy: Earn from kills/objectives • Spend on doors/perks/box • Risk vs. reward

Getting Started

  1. Load a code from Map Codes and start a custom game.
  2. Open early routes: prioritize safe loops and a perk station.
  3. Connect power when your squad has breathing room—expect a spike.
  4. Roll the Box sparingly; secure perks first.
  5. Boss prep: have escapes, burst damage, and revives planned.

Wonder Weapons

Rare pulls from the Mystery Box that redefine your kit: big crowd control, burst, or utility spikes. Save resources for perks before high‑rolling.

  • Drop rate: Rare
  • Role: Boss burn • Wave clear • Escape tools
  • Tuning: Expect iteration; check the Changelog for balance notes.

Bosses

Orisa — Sentry Protocol

Telegraphed fortify windows, line‑break charges, and lane denial. Counter with interrupts and off‑angles.

D.Va — Mech Overclock

Burst windows around micro missile volleys; punish eject timings and manage self‑destruct LOS.

Reinhardt — Shieldbreaker

Cone swings with gap‑closers; bait charges into terrain, keep revives behind cover.

Ramattra — Corruption

Phase spikes with adds and area denial. Save utility and DR for phase transitions.

FAQ

Do donations unlock in‑game benefits?

No. To respect Blizzard’s policies, donations don’t provide gameplay advantages or exclusive items.

Where do I report bugs or balance feedback?

Use the survey on the home page or open an issue on GitHub (if you’ve published the site repo).

Performance tips?

Reduce particle clutter, stick to clean loops, and avoid cross‑pulling waves during power spikes.
